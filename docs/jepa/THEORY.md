## Assumptions, coordinates and support

All vectors are centered columns. Write `x_v=A_v theta+n_v`, `n_v=B_v eta+e_v`, and `S_uv=Cov(x_u,x_v)`, `N_uv=Cov(n_u,n_v)`. Physics, nuisance and private residuals are mutually uncorrelated, and `Cov(e_c,e_t)=0`. These moment assumptions suffice for the covariance/subspace arguments; independence is stronger and is not inferred from uncorrelatedness outside a jointly Gaussian model. Gaussianity is needed when interpreting linear innovations as independent and for the stated Gaussian known-covariance Fisher formula. Assume positive-definite `Sigma_theta`, `N_cc`, `N_tt`, and full column rank `A_c` with k columns. P3 additionally requires full column rank `A_t` and zero cross-noise. P5 requires the entire retained joint noise matrix positive definite. Rank/conditioning and canonical separation are measured rather than inferred from nominal dimensions.

An encoder `z=W x` returns row functionals; reference columns are `N_cc^{-1} A_c`, so compare `span(W.T)` to this column space. For a coordinate change `x'=T x`, the raw encoder is `W' T` (raw direction columns `T.T W'.T`), not `T^{-1} W'.T`. Angles are Euclidean principal angles after this pullback; noise-metric angles are a different quantity. Use matrix residual tolerance 1e-8 and maximum subspace angle 1e-6 radians for exact tests.

Covariance support is part of the generating law: waveform `N_actual=N_raw+epsilon_dc I` with the fixed reference-derived white variance in §4.1. Its draws must actually enter every event/noise record; an inverse floor alone is not this model. Take the marginal of retained coordinates before oracle whitening; a conditional covariance obtained by conditioning on discarded samples is a different experiment. With Gaussian, parameter-independent noise the local Fisher information is `J.T solve(N_actual,J)`. Only full-rank Fisher matrices have the declared inverse CRLB. The amplitude-only known-other-parameters bound is `1/I_aa`, generally different from `(I^{-1})_aa`.

## P1: cross-covariance and displacement

Expanding `E[x_t x_c.T]` and deleting precisely the zero cross moments gives `S_tc=A_t Sigma_theta A_c.T+B_t Sigma_eta B_c.T`. If factors are correlated, terms such as `A_t Cov(theta,eta) B_c.T` remain; dropping them would invalidate the assertion. Canonical correlations are singular values of `S_cc^{-1/2} S_ct S_tt^{-1/2}`. The SVD generally mixes physical and nuisance loadings. In a separable diagonal construction with unit total marginal variances, physical cross-correlation .01 and nuisance cross-correlation .99, a one-dimensional CCA representation selects the nuisance coordinate. This establishes “can displace,” not a universal monotonic ranking under arbitrary overlapping loadings. Tests also deliberately correlate factors and verify that the reduced P1 formula then fails.

## Hard-isotropy prediction and P2

For full-rank context `W_c`, isotropic target `W_t S_tt W_t.T=I_k`, and unrestricted linear predictor P, least squares gives `P*=W_t S_tc W_c.T (W_c S_cc W_c.T)^{-1}`. The minimized MSE is `k-tr(W_t S_tc W_c.T (W_c S_cc W_c.T)^{-1} W_c S_ct W_t.T)`. Whitening context row coordinates does not change this quantity. Optimizing both k-dimensional spaces is a truncated singular-value problem with value `k-sum_{i<=k} rho_i^2`. The top context canonical space follows when selected correlations are nonzero; uniqueness requires a boundary gap. An exact tie permits multiple optimal spaces. Neither this global argument nor a change of row basis proves GD convergence.

For invertible per-view T_c,T_t, transformed whitened cross-covariance is `Q_c C Q_t.T`, where `Q_c=(T_c S_cc T_c.T)^{-1/2} T_c S_cc^{1/2}` is orthogonal, and similarly for target. Thus singular values and mapped canonical spaces are invariant (up to nonunique bases at ties). Ridge, weighted reconstruction losses and neural objectives do not inherit this assertion automatically. For a scalar complex spectral band, equal signal powers S and equal noise powers J in both views and uncorrelated signal/noise imply coherency `(c_s S+c_n J)/(S+J)`; MSC is its squared modulus. Unequal powers require the general cross-spectrum divided by the square root of marginal spectra.

## P3: no cross-noise

Under P3 assumptions `S_ct=A_c Sigma_theta A_t.T` has rank k, hence `range(S_cc^{-1} S_ct)=range(S_cc^{-1} A_c)`. The nonzero canonical context directions span this range. Woodbury gives `S_cc^{-1} A_c=N_cc^{-1} A_c (I+Sigma_theta A_c.T N_cc^{-1} A_c)^{-1}`. The right factor is invertible, proving equality with `R_c`. The k-th correlation must be positive and separated from the discarded zero spectrum. Numerical near-singularity can make finite-sample angles poor even when algebraic ranks equal k.

## P4: pure noise objective and its limitation

For `W S_cc W.T=I_k`, set `U=W S_cc^{1/2}`. Rayleigh–Ritz minimizes `tr(U S_cc^{-1/2} N_cc S_cc^{-1/2} U.T)` over `U U.T=I_k`. Equivalently use the k smallest generalized eigenvectors `N_cc v=lambda S_cc v`. Noise-whiten the signal as `H=N_cc^{-1/2} A_c Sigma_theta^{1/2}`. The corresponding whitened generalized problem has eigenvalues `1/(1+s_i(H)^2)<1` on `range(H)` and 1 on its complement. Pullback gives `span(N_cc^{-1} A_c)`. The positive smallest signal singular value gives a nonzero separation from the complement. If calibration covariance differs from event noise, this proof no longer identifies the event reference.

For independent, zero-mean overlay xi of covariance N_cc and a linear encoder, `E||W(x+xi)-Wx||^2=tr(W N_cc W.T)`. Deep encoders, normalization by embedding covariance, and soft SIGReg change the objective.

With both context and target hard isotropy, let `T=S_cc^{-1/2} S_ct S_tt^{-1/2}` and `Nw=S_cc^{-1/2} N_cc S_cc^{-1/2}`. For any row-orthonormal U_c, the target's k-dimensional row space can contain `row(U_c T)` because its rank is at most k and d_t>=k. Eliminating the predictor and maximizing over target space therefore gives the exact reduced objective `k-tr[U_c (T T.T-beta Nw) U_c.T]`. Its global solution uses the k largest eigenvectors of this symmetric matrix. Rank deficiency in `U_c T` permits a nonunique orthonormal target completion and zero predictor singular values; it does not invalidate the optimum or either isotropy constraint. A boundary eigentie permits nonunique context optima. A deterministic completion and tie convention belong in the reviewed solver config. This reduction avoids claiming convergence of an arbitrary iterative optimizer.

A finite beta does not give exact P4 recovery. Consider k=1, `S_cc=S_tt=I_2`, `A_c=A_t=(.1,0).T`, unit physics variance, `N_cc=N_tt=diag(.99,1)`, `N_ct=diag(0,.99)`. This joint noise covariance is SPD and all factors can be independent Gaussians. Then `S_ct=diag(.01,.99)`. With **both** encoders hard-isotropic and optimized scalar predictor, the objective is `1-(w_c S_ct w_t.T)^2+beta w_c N_cc w_c.T`. For fixed unit w_c the optimum target aligns with `S_tc w_c.T`; minimizing is the top eigenvector of `S_ct S_tc-beta N_cc`. At beta .1, 1 and 10 its unique winner is nuisance: score difference `.9800-.01 beta>0`. Pure GEVP still selects physics. This is an exact global finite-beta negative control, not optimizer failure and not G1.

## P5: conditional triangular oracle

Partition the retained SPD joint noise covariance as `[N_cc,N_ct;N_tc,N_tt]`. Its lower Cholesky has blocks `[L_c,0;K,L_s]`, with `K=N_tc L_c^{-T}` and `L_s L_s.T=N_tt-N_tc N_cc^{-1}N_ct`. The oracle gives `y_c=L_c^{-1}x_c` and `y_t=L_s^{-1}(x_t-N_tc N_cc^{-1}x_c)`. Thus noise covariance is identity, context depends only on raw context, and residual target signal is `D=A_t-N_tc N_cc^{-1} A_c`. When D retains rank k, P3 in transformed coordinates and pullback recover `R_c`; no claim equates transformed correlations to their original no-sharing counterparts.

Eligibility requires context and D smallest/largest singular-value ratios >1e-8, k-th population canonical correlation >1e-8 and retained/discarded correlation gap >1e-8 (a nonexistent discarded mode has correlation zero). Record ratios and both canonical margins. Singular joint covariance is rejected before Cholesky, even if both marginals are positive definite. Do not introduce jitter.

The registered annihilation fixture has `A_c=(1,0).T`, `A_t=(.25,0).T`, `N_cc=diag(2,1)`, `N_tt=diag(1.25,1)`, `N_ct=diag(.5,0)`. Joint noise is SPD but D=0: output is `oracle_rank_ineligible`. A full-rank original target alone is insufficient. This reproduces the logical content of `reviews/check_p5_counterexample.py`, a disclosed prior development counterexample.

## Criterion M and scope of external claims

M is the k-th singular value of event-normalized estimated cross-signal minus the top regularized noise canonical singular value, using independent event/noise calibration, fixed seed-2718 QR projections, and `epsilon(S)=1e-3 trace(S)/d`, exactly as §2.3. It is not signal-only CCA, can exceed the usual correlation range through finite estimation, and gives no recovery theorem. Calibration mismatch invalidates a physical subtraction interpretation. No probe-test labels or data select M.

The runbook's LeJEPA/SIGReg citation concerns soft regularization; Littwin et al. concerns tied stop-gradient deep-linear dynamics under its own assumptions. Neither is used as an imported premise of the proofs above, which are direct linear algebra. Full-text literature reproduction and SIGReg source pinning remain later work; this note makes no claim that random-loading dynamics meet those papers' hypotheses.

## Source/configuration gate: waveform nuisance law

The final execution review explicitly leaves line bandwidth/normalization and common-mode construction for source/config review. `src/latent_monitor/tier1.py` supplies a normalized composite reference PSD (function `default_noise_base`), default corr_strength=.4 (`reference_cell`), and a different `sigma_covariance_cells` line: .08 fs, width .004 fs, scale 12, inserted into a normalized composite. That existing perturbation is not the requested additive independent I/Q law at the template-selected frequency. It cannot be imported as an equivalent default. No two temporal basis vectors for the added common mode are declared there.

Unresolved before E1b/E2 generation: line bandwidth and PSD normalization; channel gains/phases and coefficient covariance; whether “common-mode strength” scales amplitude, variance or correlation; which temporal covariance defines its two leading vectors and how repeated leading eigenvalues are handled; exact mapping of reference white-component variance after composite normalization; and the fixed common-mode level used when selecting E2's three line levels. These require a recorded G5 amendment or author-approved config resolution and G6 validation; no outcomes should select these values.

The following historical proposal is retained to show the design discussion. Original proposal: use a zero-bandwidth random sinusoid at the registered template-selected non-DC bin, with identical unit channel gains and zero relative phase. Let independent I,Q be N(0,v_line); set `line[c,t]=I cos(2 pi b t/256)+Q sin(2 pi b t/256)`, so every sample has variance v_line and the temporal covariance is `v_line (cc.T+ss.T)`. Define v_line as grid multiplier times the mean raw-coordinate diagonal covariance attributable to the reference white PSD component **after the reference composite normalization**, before the declared extra white addition. Keep all reference components fixed.

For an additive common mode, use the leading two orthonormal eigenvectors of the reference single-channel temporal covariance, ordered decreasing eigenvalue, with sign fixed by first maximal-magnitude coordinate positive. Resolve exact degenerate eigenspaces deterministically by projected coordinate axes in ascending index and Gram–Schmidt (report the degeneracy); do not let library eigenbasis choices set the experiment. Normalize each temporal vector to mean square one, use identical unit channel gains, and draw two independent Gaussian coefficients with variance `g*v_white/2` each, where g is the declared common-mode-strength grid. This proposal interprets strength as added variance rather than correlation. For the line-only sweep hold g=0; for the common-only sweep hold v_line=0; use fixed g=.45 for E2 line-level selection and all E2 cells, so that the optional E4 common-mode-amplitude fault acts on a nonzero component.
