## Feature and probe provenance of the committed particle suite (T3.3)

- Committed particle tasks (`ext_r2.metrics_from_streams`, `ext_r2.py:1105-1131`) use the **all-mean token feature** of each μ/e event (`la._mean_tokens(tokens, None)`, `ext_r2.py:1040`): μ/e AUC by `ext_followup._logistic_auc` (`ext_r2.py:1110`); direction by `ext_followup._direction_tasks`, reported as its MLP median angle (`ext_r2.py:1117-1123`); energy by `ext_followup._energy_task`, linear R² of `deposited_MeV` on LUCiD-contained events (`ext_r2.py:1124-1131`).
- Committed vertex readouts (`ext_r2_repair_probes.evaluate`, `ext_r2_repair_probes.py:171-215`): Ridge on the `VERTEX_FORMS` (incl. `spatial_moment_latent` = Appendix A.2) and the MLP on `spatial_moment_full` with probe seed = run seed.
- New here (READOUT_SPEC_v1): direction, energy (ln E, resolution metrics) and PID on the spatial-moment latent (A.2) features instead of the all-mean; the committed MLP direction probe is kept for continuity.
